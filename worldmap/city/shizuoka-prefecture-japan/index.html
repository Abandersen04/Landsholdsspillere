Football players from Shizuoka Prefecture

Japan 209 players 1894–2004 195 men · 14 women

Shizuoka Prefecture is a city in Japan with 209 notable football players born there according to Wikidata. This includes 195 male and 14 female players. Players were born between 1894–2004. The most prominent are Tadao Horie, Naoto Otake, Yoshinori Ishigami, Masaru Uchiyama and Seki Matsunaga.
