Football players born in Mykolaiv

Russian Empire 74 players 1890–1998

Mykolaiv is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Nikita Rukavytsya, but the city has produced 74 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Vladlen Yurchenko, Serhiy Krakovskyi, Saşa Yunisoğlu and Vyacheslav Checher. Documented players span more than 108 years, from 1890 to 1998.

Players from Mykolaiv have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
