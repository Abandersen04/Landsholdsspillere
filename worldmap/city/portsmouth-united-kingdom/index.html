Football players born in Portsmouth

United Kingdom 114 players 1861–2004 106 men · 8 women

Portsmouth is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain, but the city has produced 114 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Mason Mount, James Ward-Prowse, Jack Hobbs and Steve Foster. Among them are 106 male and 8 female players. Documented players span more than 143 years, from 1861 to 2004.

Players from Portsmouth have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
