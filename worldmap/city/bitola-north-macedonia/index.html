Football players born in Bitola

North Macedonia 40 players 1922–2002

Bitola is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Miljan Miljanić, but the city has produced 40 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Stefan Spirovski, Kire Ristevski, Nikolče Noveski and Georgi Hristov. Birth years range from 1922 to 2002.
