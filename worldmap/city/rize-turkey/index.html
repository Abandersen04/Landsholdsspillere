Turkey 82 players 1900–2004 80 men · 1 women

Rize, Turkey, is a notable football birthplace — 82 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Zafer Biryol, Yasin Çakmak and Birol Parlak. Among them are 80 male and 1 female players. Documented players span more than 104 years, from 1900 to 2004.
