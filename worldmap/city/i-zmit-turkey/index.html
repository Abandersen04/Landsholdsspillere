Football players born in İzmit

Turkey 60 players 1906–2007 56 men · 4 women

İzmit, Turkey, is a notable football birthplace — 60 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Kerem Aktürkoğlu, Ali Beratlıgil and Batuhan Altıntaş. Among them are 56 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 101 years, from 1906 to 2007.
