Football players born in Kobe

Japan 48 players 1913–2006

Kobe, Japan, is a notable football birthplace — 48 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Shinji Kagawa, Gen Shōji and Ryoichi Maeda. Documented players span more than 93 years, from 1913 to 2006.
