Football players born in Vukovar

Croatia 23 players 1920–2004

Vukovar is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Siniša Mihajlović, but the city has produced 23 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Aleksandar Čavrić, Dario Krešić, Milan Gajić and Ante Miše. Documented players span more than 84 years, from 1920 to 2004.
