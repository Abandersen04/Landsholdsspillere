Football players born in Milan

Italy 644 players 1870–2008 615 men · 29 women

Milan is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Paolo Maldini, but the city has produced 644 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Giuseppe Meazza, Mattia De Sciglio, Riccardo Montolivo and Walter Zenga. Among them are 615 male and 29 female players. Documented players span more than 138 years, from 1870 to 2008.

Players from Milan have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
