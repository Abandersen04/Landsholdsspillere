Japan 279 players 1908–2007 255 men · 24 women

This page lists all 279 football players born in Osaka Prefecture that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Masaaki Higashiguchi, Yoichiro Kakitani, Takahiro Ogihara and Akira Kitaguchi. Among them are 255 male and 24 female players. Documented players span more than 99 years, from 1908 to 2007.
