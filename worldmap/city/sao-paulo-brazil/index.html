Football players born in São Paulo

Brazil 1164 players 1874–2007 1123 men · 41 women

São Paulo, Brazil, is one of the world's most prolific football birthplaces — 1164 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Rivelino, Gabriel Jesus and Paulinho. Among them are 1123 male and 41 female players. Documented players span more than 133 years, from 1874 to 2007.
