Germany 147 players 1903–2007 129 men · 18 women

Essen, Germany, is a significant source of football talent — 147 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Jens Lehmann, Leroy Sané and Helmut Rahn. Among them are 129 male and 18 female players. Documented players span more than 104 years, from 1903 to 2007.
