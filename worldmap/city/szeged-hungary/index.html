Hungary 84 players 1880–2004 72 men · 12 women

This page lists all 84 football players born in Szeged that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Károly Sándor, Lajos Kocsis, Imre Szabics and Lajos Korányi. Among them are 72 male and 12 female players. Documented players span more than 124 years, from 1880 to 2004.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
