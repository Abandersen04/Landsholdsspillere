Football players born in Dębica

Poland 21 players 1909–2000

Dębica is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Artur Jędrzejczyk, but the city has produced 21 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Seweryn Gancarczyk, Paweł Bochniewicz, Leszek Pisz and Paweł Magdoń. Documented players span more than 91 years, from 1909 to 2000.
