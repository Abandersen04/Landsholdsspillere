Football players born in Barakaldo

Spain 96 players 1900–2008 85 men · 11 women

Barakaldo is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Javier Clemente, but the city has produced 96 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jacinto Quincoces, Yeray Álvarez, Asier del Horno and José Ramón Alexanko. Among them are 85 male and 11 female players. Documented players span more than 108 years, from 1900 to 2008.

Players from Barakaldo have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
