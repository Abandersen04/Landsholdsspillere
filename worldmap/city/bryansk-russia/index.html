Football players born in Bryansk

Russia 36 players 1939–2006

Bryansk is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Andriy Nesmachniy, but the city has produced 36 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Vladimir Astapovsky, Valeri Petrakov, Sergei Terekhov and Yevgeni Shlyakov. Birth years range from 1939 to 2006.
