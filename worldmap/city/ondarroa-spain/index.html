Spain 30 players 1901–2004 26 men · 4 women

Ondarroa, Spain, is a notable football birthplace — 30 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Kepa Arrizabalaga, Iñigo Martínez and Eñaut Zubikarai. Among them are 26 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 103 years, from 1901 to 2004.
