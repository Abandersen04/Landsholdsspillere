Football players born in Mainz

Germany 45 players 1895–2006 41 men · 4 women

Mainz is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Mario Pašalić, but the city has produced 45 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Ridle Baku, Franco Foda, Josephine Henning and Sandro Schwarz. Among them are 41 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 111 years, from 1895 to 2006.
