Football players from Hyōgo Prefecture

Japan 173 players 1903–2007 156 men · 17 women

Hyōgo Prefecture is a city in Japan with 173 notable football players born there according to Wikidata. This includes 156 male and 17 female players. Players were born between 1903–2007. The most prominent are Shōichi Nishimura, Akihiro Ienaga, Shozo Tsugitani, Ichiji Otani and Tokizo Ichihashi.
