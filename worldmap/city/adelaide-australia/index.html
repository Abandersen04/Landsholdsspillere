Australia 112 players 1884–2008 93 men · 19 women

Adelaide, Australia, is a significant source of football talent — 112 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Aurelio Vidmar, Anthony LaPaglia and John Aloisi. Among them are 93 male and 19 female players. Documented players span more than 124 years, from 1884 to 2008.
