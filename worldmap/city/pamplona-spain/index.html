Football players born in Pamplona

Spain 180 players 1895–2007 166 men · 14 women

This page lists all 180 football players born in Pamplona that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Fernando Llorente, Nico Williams, Nacho Monreal and Mikel Merino. Among them are 166 male and 14 female players. Documented players span more than 112 years, from 1895 to 2007.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
