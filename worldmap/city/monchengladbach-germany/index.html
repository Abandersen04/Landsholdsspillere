Football players born in Mönchengladbach

Germany 62 players 1889–2007 52 men · 10 women

Mönchengladbach is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Marc-André ter Stegen, but the city has produced 62 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jupp Heynckes, Marcell Jansen, Günter Netzer and Horst-Dieter Höttges. Among them are 52 male and 10 female players. Documented players span more than 118 years, from 1889 to 2007.

Players from Mönchengladbach have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
