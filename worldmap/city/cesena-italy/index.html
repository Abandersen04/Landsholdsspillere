Football players born in Cesena

Italy 71 players 1883–2004 69 men · 2 women

Cesena is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Azeglio Vicini, but the city has produced 71 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Mattia Zaccagni, Lorenzo Minotti, Sebastiano Rossi and Davide Biondini. Among them are 69 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 121 years, from 1883 to 2004.

Players from Cesena have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
