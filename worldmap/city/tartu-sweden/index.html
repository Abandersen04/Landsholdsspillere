How many professional footballers have come from Tartu? According to Wikidata, at least 77 association football players were born here. That list includes Martin Reim, Taijo Teniste and Martin Vunk. Among them are 70 male and 7 female players. Documented players span more than 93 years, from 1910 to 2003.

Players from Tartu have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
