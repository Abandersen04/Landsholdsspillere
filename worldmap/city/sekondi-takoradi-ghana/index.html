Ghana 32 players 1935–2005 30 men · 2 women

Sekondi-Takoradi is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Kim Grant, but the city has produced 32 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Samuel Inkoom, Desmond N'Ze, Edwin Gyimah and Janet Egyir. Among them are 30 male and 2 female players. Birth years range from 1935 to 2005.
