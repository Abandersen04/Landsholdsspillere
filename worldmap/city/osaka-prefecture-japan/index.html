Football players from Osaka Prefecture

Japan 272 players 1908–2007 249 men · 23 women

Osaka Prefecture is a city in Japan with 272 notable football players born there according to Wikidata. This includes 249 male and 23 female players. Players were born between 1908–2007. The most prominent are Masaaki Higashiguchi, Yoichiro Kakitani, Takahiro Ogihara, Akira Kitaguchi and Yoshio Furukawa.
