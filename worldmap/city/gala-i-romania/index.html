Football players born in Galați

Romania 89 players 1910–2010

Galați, Romania, is a notable football birthplace — 89 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Sorin Ghionea, Alexandru Bourceanu and Dănuț Lupu. Documented players span more than 100 years, from 1910 to 2010.

The players listed here were identified through Wikidata's open knowledge base, where each person is classified as an association football player (Q937857) with Galați registered as their birthplace. The data is community-maintained and updated continuously.
