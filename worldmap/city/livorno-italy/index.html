Football players born in Livorno

Italy 138 players 1880–2004 134 men · 4 women

This page lists all 138 football players born in Livorno that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Massimiliano Allegri, Cristiano Lucarelli, Leonardo Pavoletti and Armando Picchi. Among them are 134 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 124 years, from 1880 to 2004.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
