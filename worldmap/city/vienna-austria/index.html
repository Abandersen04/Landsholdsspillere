Austria 797 players 1865–2008 767 men · 30 women

How many professional footballers have come from Vienna? According to Wikidata, at least 797 association football players were born here. That list includes David Alaba, Marko Arnautović and Ernst Happel. Among them are 767 male and 30 female players. Documented players span more than 143 years, from 1865 to 2008.

Players from Vienna have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
