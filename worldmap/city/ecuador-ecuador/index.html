Ecuador, Ecuador, is a notable football birthplace — 34 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Geovanny Nazareno, Nixon Carcelén and Augusto Batioja. Among them are 31 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 107 years, from 1901 to 2008.
