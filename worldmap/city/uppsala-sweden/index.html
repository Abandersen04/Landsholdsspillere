Football players born in Uppsala

Sweden 43 players 1884–2007 33 men · 10 women

Uppsala, Sweden, is a notable football birthplace — 43 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Linda Sembrant, Filippa Angeldal and Nathalie Björn. Among them are 33 male and 10 female players. Documented players span more than 123 years, from 1884 to 2007.
