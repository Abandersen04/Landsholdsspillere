Netherlands 77 players 1876–2007 74 men · 3 women

Arnhem, Netherlands, is a notable football birthplace — 77 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Davy Pröpper, Ferdi Kadıoğlu and Andy van der Meyde. Among them are 74 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 131 years, from 1876 to 2007.
