Football players born in Alphen aan den Rijn

Netherlands 15 players 1911–2004 13 men · 2 women

Alphen aan den Rijn is best known in football circles as the birthplace of John Heitinga, but the city has produced 15 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jens Toornstra, Kerstin Casparij, Mats Knoester and Jaap van der Leck. Among them are 13 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 93 years, from 1911 to 2004.
