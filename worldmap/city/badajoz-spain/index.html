Football players born in Badajoz

Spain 41 players 1897–2008 38 men · 3 women

Badajoz is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Fernando Pacheco Flores, but the city has produced 41 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Adelardo Rodríguez, Javi Galán, Joaquín Vázquez and Jesús Rueda. Among them are 38 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 111 years, from 1897 to 2008.
