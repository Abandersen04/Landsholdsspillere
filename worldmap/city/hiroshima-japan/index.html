Football players born in Hiroshima

Japan 134 players 1899–2006 132 men · 2 women

Hiroshima, Japan, is a significant source of football talent — 134 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Masato Morishige, Hiroaki Morishima and Tomoaki Makino. Among them are 132 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 107 years, from 1899 to 2006.
