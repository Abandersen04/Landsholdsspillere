Football players born in South Shields

United Kingdom 107 players 1873–2003 106 men · 1 women

South Shields is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Phil Brown, but the city has produced 107 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Stan Mortensen, Ramón Zabalo, Blair Adams and Steve Simonsen. Among them are 106 male and 1 female players. Documented players span more than 130 years, from 1873 to 2003.

Players from South Shields have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
