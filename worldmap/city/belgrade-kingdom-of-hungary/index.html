Football players born in Belgrade

Kingdom of Hungary 837 players 1875–2008 835 men · 2 women

Belgrade is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Dušan Vlahović, but the city has produced 837 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Dejan Stanković, Marko Pantelić, Antonio Rukavina and Dušan Petković. Among them are 835 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 133 years, from 1875 to 2008.

Players from Belgrade have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
