France 460 players 1873–2009 435 men · 25 women

460 association football players born in Paris, France, are recorded on Wikidata — among them Kylian Mbappé, N'Golo Kanté and Kingsley Coman. Among them are 435 male and 25 female players. Documented players span more than 136 years, from 1873 to 2009.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
