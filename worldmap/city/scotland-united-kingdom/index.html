Football players born in Scotland

United Kingdom 539 players 1847–2006 518 men · 21 women

Scotland is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Joe Jordan, but the city has produced 539 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Aaron Hickey, Callan Elliot, Jimmy Mackay and John Coyle. Among them are 518 male and 21 female players. Documented players span more than 159 years, from 1847 to 2006.

Players from Scotland have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
