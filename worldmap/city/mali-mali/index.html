Football players born in Mali

Mali 89 players 1967–2006 83 men · 6 women

Mali is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Hamary Traoré, but the city has produced 89 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Cheick Doucouré, Mohamed Camara, Lassana Coulibaly and Daouda Guindo. Among them are 83 male and 6 female players. Birth years range from 1967 to 2006.
