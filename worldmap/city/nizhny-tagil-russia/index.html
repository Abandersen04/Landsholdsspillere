Football players born in Nizhny Tagil

Russia 23 players 1913–1999

Nizhny Tagil is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Oleg Shatov, but the city has produced 23 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Maksim Kanunnikov, Arsen Adamov, Yuri Kolomyts and Yuri Matveyev. Documented players span more than 86 years, from 1913 to 1999.
