Football players born in Kraków

Poland 361 players 1871–2007 359 men · 2 women

Kraków is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Marcin Wasilewski, but the city has produced 361 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Krzysztof Mączyński, Adam Buksa, Adam Nawałka and Robert Gadocha. Among them are 359 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 136 years, from 1871 to 2007.

Players from Kraków have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
