Denmark 53 players 1866–2006 49 men · 4 women

This page lists all 53 football players born in Aalborg that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Ebbe Sand, Kasper Hjulmand, Thomas Enevoldsen and Lucas Andersen. Among them are 49 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 140 years, from 1866 to 2006.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
