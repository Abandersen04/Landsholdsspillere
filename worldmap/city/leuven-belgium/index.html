Football players born in Leuven

Belgium 39 players 1881–2005 32 men · 7 women

Leuven is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Dries Mertens, but the city has produced 39 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Dennis Praet, Cyriel Dessers, Denis Odoi and Rafik Belghali. Among them are 32 male and 7 female players. Documented players span more than 124 years, from 1881 to 2005.
