This page lists all 111 football players born in New York City that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Gōtoku Sakai, Folarin Balogun, Dan Calichman and Yuka Momiki. Among them are 103 male and 8 female players. Documented players span more than 135 years, from 1873 to 2008.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
