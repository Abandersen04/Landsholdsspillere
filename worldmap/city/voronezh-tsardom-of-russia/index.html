Football players born in Voronezh

Tsardom of Russia 112 players 1920–2004 106 men · 6 women

Voronezh is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Roman Shishkin, but the city has produced 112 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Aleksandr Borodyuk, Aleksey Nikolaev, Elena Danilova and Dmitri Skopintsev. Among them are 106 male and 6 female players. Documented players span more than 84 years, from 1920 to 2004.
