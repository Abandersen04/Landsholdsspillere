Football players born in Patras

Greece 41 players 1876–2004

Patras is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Andreas Samaris, but the city has produced 41 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Giorgos Kyriakopoulos, Yannis Masouras, Spiros Livathinos and George Marshall. Documented players span more than 128 years, from 1876 to 2004.
