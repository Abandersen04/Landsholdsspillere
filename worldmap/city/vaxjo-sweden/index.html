Football players born in Växjö

Sweden 27 players 1936–1999 26 men · 1 women

Växjö is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Joachim Björklund, but the city has produced 27 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Tommy Svensson, Harry Bild, Ulrik Jansson and Markus Jonsson. Among them are 26 male and 1 female players. Birth years range from 1936 to 1999.
