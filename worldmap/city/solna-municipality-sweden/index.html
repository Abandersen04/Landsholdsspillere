Football players born in Solna Municipality

Sweden 26 players 1925–2008 24 men · 2 women

Solna Municipality is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Anders Limpar, but the city has produced 26 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Rami Shaaban, Henok Goitom, Martin Mutumba and Magnus Eriksson. Among them are 24 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 83 years, from 1925 to 2008.
