Albacete, Spain, is a notable football birthplace — 45 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Sisinio González Martínez, Santi Denia and Rodrigo Zalazar. Among them are 43 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 88 years, from 1917 to 2005.
