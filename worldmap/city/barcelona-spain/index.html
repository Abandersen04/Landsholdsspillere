Football players born in Barcelona

Spain 668 players 1867–2010 622 men · 46 women

Barcelona, Spain, is one of the world's most prolific football birthplaces — 668 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Gerard Piqué, Héctor Bellerín and Marcos Llorente. Among them are 622 male and 46 female players. Documented players span more than 143 years, from 1867 to 2010.
