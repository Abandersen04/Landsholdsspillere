Estonia, Estonia, is a notable football birthplace — 21 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Rain Vessenberg, Aleksandr Olerski and Daniela Mona Lambin. The dataset includes a notably high share of female players: 12 women and 9 men. Documented players span more than 99 years, from 1897 to 1996.
