Football players born in Bruges

Belgium 94 players 1876–2001

Bruges is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Charles De Ketelaere, but the city has produced 94 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Nicolas Lombaerts, Thomas Buffel, Hector Goetinck and Philippe Vande Walle. Documented players span more than 125 years, from 1876 to 2001.
