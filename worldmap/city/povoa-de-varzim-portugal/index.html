Football players born in Póvoa de Varzim

Portugal 42 players 1952–2005

Póvoa de Varzim is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Bruno Alves, but the city has produced 42 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Luís Neto, António Lima Pereira, Francisco Augusto Neto Ramos and Geraldo Alves. Birth years range from 1952 to 2005.
