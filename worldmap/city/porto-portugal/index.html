Portugal 164 players 1874–2008 161 men · 3 women

This page lists all 164 football players born in Porto that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Diogo Jota, Raul Meireles, André Villas-Boas and João Mário. Among them are 161 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 134 years, from 1874 to 2008.
