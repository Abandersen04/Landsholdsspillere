Slovakia 198 players 1908–2005 179 men · 19 women

This page lists all 198 football players born in Bratislava that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Ľubomír Luhový, Róbert Vittek, Vladimír Weiss and Lukas Hradecky. Among them are 179 male and 19 female players. Documented players span more than 97 years, from 1908 to 2005.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
