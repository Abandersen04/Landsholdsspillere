Croatia 370 players 1893–2007 367 men · 3 women

How many professional footballers have come from Split? According to Wikidata, at least 370 association football players were born here. That list includes Ivan Perišić, Ivica Vastić and Slaven Bilić. Among them are 367 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 114 years, from 1893 to 2007.

Players from Split have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
