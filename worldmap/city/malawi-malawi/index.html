Malawi, Malawi, is a notable football birthplace — 33 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Tabitha Chawinga, Temwa Chawinga and Joshua Kitolano. The dataset includes a notably high share of female players: 14 women and 19 men. Birth years range from 1970 to 2006.
