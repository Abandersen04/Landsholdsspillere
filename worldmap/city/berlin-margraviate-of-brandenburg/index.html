Football players born in Berlin

Margraviate of Brandenburg 589 players 1870–2008 552 men · 37 women

Berlin is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Antonio Rüdiger, but the city has produced 589 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Muhamed Bešić, John Brooks, Hakan Balta and Maximilian Mittelstädt. Among them are 552 male and 37 female players. Documented players span more than 138 years, from 1870 to 2008.

Players from Berlin have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
