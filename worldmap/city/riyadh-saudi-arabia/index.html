Football players born in Riyadh

Saudi Arabia 119 players 1946–2003 118 men · 1 women

Riyadh is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Salem Al-Dawsari, but the city has produced 119 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Salman Al-Faraj, Sami Al-Jaber, Saeed Al-Owairan and Nawaf Al Abed. Among them are 118 male and 1 female players. Birth years range from 1946 to 2003.
