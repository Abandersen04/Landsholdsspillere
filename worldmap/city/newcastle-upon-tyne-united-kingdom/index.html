Football players born in Newcastle upon Tyne

United Kingdom 311 players 1851–2008 308 men · 3 women

Newcastle upon Tyne is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Alan Shearer, but the city has produced 311 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jack Colback, Paul Dummett, Adam Armstrong and Sammy Ameobi. Among them are 308 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 157 years, from 1851 to 2008.

Players from Newcastle upon Tyne have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
