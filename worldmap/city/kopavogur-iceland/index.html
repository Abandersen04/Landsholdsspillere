Kópavogur is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Sverrir Ingi Ingason, but the city has produced 14 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jón Dagur Þorsteinsson, Patrik Gunnarsson, Davíð Kristján Ólafsson and Guðmundur Kristjánsson. Among them are 13 male and 1 female players. Most players were born between 1980 and 2001.
