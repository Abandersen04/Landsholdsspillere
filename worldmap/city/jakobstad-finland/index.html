Finland 27 players 1916–2004 25 men · 2 women

Jakobstad, Finland, is a notable football birthplace — 27 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Jonas Portin, Sebastian Mannström and Jessica Julin. Among them are 25 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 88 years, from 1916 to 2004.
