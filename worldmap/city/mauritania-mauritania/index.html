Mauritania, Mauritania, is a notable football birthplace — 37 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Dialo Guidileye, Sayed Ali Bechir and Mohamed Soueïd. The dataset includes a notably high share of female players: 15 women and 22 men. Birth years range from 1964 to 1999.
