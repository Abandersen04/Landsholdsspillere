This page lists all 70 football players born in Algeria that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Mohamed Chaïb, Yacine Titraoui, Fawzi Benkhalidi and Larbi El Hadi. Among them are 62 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 90 years, from 1913 to 2003.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
