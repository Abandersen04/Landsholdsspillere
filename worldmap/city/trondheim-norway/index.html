Norway 122 players 1885–2008 116 men · 6 women

This page lists all 122 football players born in Trondheim that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Alexander Sørloth, Rune Bratseth, Per Ciljan Skjelbred and Arnfinn Bergmann. Among them are 116 male and 6 female players. Documented players span more than 123 years, from 1885 to 2008.
