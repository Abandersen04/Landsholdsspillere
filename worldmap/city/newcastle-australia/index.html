Football players born in Newcastle

Australia 44 players 1902–2007 34 men · 10 women

Newcastle is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Connor Metcalfe, but the city has produced 44 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Clayton Zane, Colin Curran, Emily van Egmond and Robbie Middleby. Among them are 34 male and 10 female players. Documented players span more than 105 years, from 1902 to 2007.
