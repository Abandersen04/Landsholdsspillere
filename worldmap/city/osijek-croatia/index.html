Croatia 123 players 1894–2009 122 men · 1 women

This page lists all 123 football players born in Osijek that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Davor Šuker, Domagoj Vida, Igor Cvitanović and Borna Barišić. Among them are 122 male and 1 female players. Documented players span more than 115 years, from 1894 to 2009.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
