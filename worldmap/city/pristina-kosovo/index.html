Football players born in Pristina

Kosovo 105 players 1950–2000 99 men · 5 women

Pristina is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Lorik Cana, but the city has produced 105 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Besart Berisha, Etrit Berisha, Ermir Lenjani and Amir Rrahmani. Among them are 99 male and 5 female players. Birth years range from 1950 to 2000.

Players from Pristina have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
