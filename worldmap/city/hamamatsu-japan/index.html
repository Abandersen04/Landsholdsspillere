Hamamatsu, Japan, is a notable football birthplace — 54 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Kisho Yano, Hiroki Yamada and Shigetatsu Matsunaga. Among them are 52 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 92 years, from 1915 to 2007.

The players listed here were identified through Wikidata's open knowledge base, where each person is classified as an association football player (Q937857) with Hamamatsu registered as their birthplace. The data is community-maintained and updated continuously.
