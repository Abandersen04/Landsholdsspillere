About this map
This world football map shows the birthplaces of over 263,000 association football players from every corner of the globe. Each pin represents a city — click it to see which players were born there, filter by nationality, notability or era, and follow links to individual Wikipedia articles.

The map reveals patterns in global football geography: South American capitals like Buenos Aires and Montevideo have produced thousands of players; European cities like Glasgow, Liverpool and Budapest dominated in the early 20th century; and modern football's reach extends to cities in Africa, Asia and the Pacific.

About the data:
All players are sourced from Wikidata. Each player is classified as an association football player (Q937857) with a known birthplace.

Known limitations:
  • Missing players: if a player lacks a birthplace or football classification on Wikidata, they will not appear here
  • Wrong birthplace: some players are pinned to the wrong city due to errors in Wikidata
  • Missing images or birth years: not all players have complete data on Wikidata
  • English-language bias: English Wikipedia has more editors covering lower-league English and British football than equivalent leagues elsewhere — so the UK has disproportionately many players in the dataset relative to its actual global football output

Errors can be corrected directly on wikidata.org.
