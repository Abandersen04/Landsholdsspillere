Football players born in Frankfurt

Francia 188 players 1888–2008 175 men · 13 women

Frankfurt is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Emre Can, but the city has produced 188 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Niklas Süle, Andreas Möller, Birgit Prinz and Jermaine Jones. Among them are 175 male and 13 female players. Documented players span more than 120 years, from 1888 to 2008.

Players from Frankfurt have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
