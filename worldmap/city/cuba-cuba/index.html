Cuba, Cuba, is a notable football birthplace — 40 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Pedro Ferrer, Joaquín Arias and Carlos Oliveira. Among them are 38 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 97 years, from 1908 to 2005.
