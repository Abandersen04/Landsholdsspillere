Football players born in Warsaw

Poland 261 players 1874–2005 258 men · 3 women

Warsaw is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Robert Lewandowski, but the city has produced 261 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Wojciech Szczęsny, Michał Żewłakow, Mateusz Wieteska and Marcin Żewłakow. Among them are 258 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 131 years, from 1874 to 2005.
