Football players born in Gothenburg

Sweden 259 players 1867–2008 252 men · 7 women

Gothenburg is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Anders Svensson, but the city has produced 259 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Gunnar Gren, Marcus Allbäck, Kári Árnason and Gustav Svensson. Among them are 252 male and 7 female players. Documented players span more than 141 years, from 1867 to 2008.

Players from Gothenburg have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
