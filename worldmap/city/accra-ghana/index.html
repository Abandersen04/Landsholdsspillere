Football players born in Accra

Ghana 542 players 1865–2008 529 men · 13 women

This page lists all 542 football players born in Accra that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Michael Essien, Marcel Desailly, Asamoah Gyan and Kwadwo Asamoah. Among them are 529 male and 13 female players. Documented players span more than 143 years, from 1865 to 2008.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
