Football players born in Kinshasa

Democratic Republic of the Congo 369 players 1928–2008 349 men · 20 women

Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, is a significant source of football talent — 369 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Claude Makelélé, Steve Mandanda and Christian Benteke. Among them are 349 male and 20 female players. Birth years range from 1928 to 2008.
