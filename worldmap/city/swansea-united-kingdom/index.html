United Kingdom 135 players 1872–2002

Swansea is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Chris Coleman, but the city has produced 135 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Joe Rodon, Jazz Richards, John Hartson and Ivor Allchurch. Documented players span more than 130 years, from 1872 to 2002.
