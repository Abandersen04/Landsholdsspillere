Cabo Frio is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Leandro Euzébio, but the city has produced 18 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include André Felipe, Daniel Silva dos Santos, José Leandro Ferreira and Rafael Navarro. Birth years range from 1959 to 2007.
