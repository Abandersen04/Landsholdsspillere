Football players born in Bucharest

Principality of Wallachia 499 players 1900–2007 497 men · 2 women

Bucharest is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Mircea Lucescu, but the city has produced 499 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Ciprian Marica, Ciprian Tătărușanu, Radu Drăgușin and Dan Petrescu. Among them are 497 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 107 years, from 1900 to 2007.

Players from Bucharest have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
