Colombia 300 players 1923–2005 292 men · 7 women

How many professional footballers have come from Medellín? According to Wikidata, at least 300 association football players were born here. That list includes René Higuita, Juan Fernando Quintero and Santiago Arias. Among them are 292 male and 7 female players. Documented players span more than 82 years, from 1923 to 2005.

Players from Medellín have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
