Football players born in Yokohama

Japan 94 players 1955–2006 88 men · 6 women

Yokohama is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Wataru Endō, but the city has produced 94 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Shunsuke Nakamura, Masayuki Okano, Yuzo Kurihara and Ryuzo Morioka. Among them are 88 male and 6 female players. Birth years range from 1955 to 2006.

Players from Yokohama have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
