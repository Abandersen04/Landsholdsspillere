Siena is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Bernardo Corradi, but the city has produced 19 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Matteo Trefoloni, Niccolò Giannetti, Pier Larrauri and Giacomo Bindi. Among them are 17 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 102 years, from 1899 to 2001.
