Football players from Aichi Prefecture

Japan 133 players 1914–2005 123 men · 10 women

Aichi Prefecture is a city in Japan with 133 notable football players born there according to Wikidata. This includes 123 male and 10 female players. Players were born between 1914–2005. The most prominent are Taizo Kawamoto, Mitsunori Yoshida, Shinichiro Tani, Tomoyuki Kajino and Yuji Sugano.
