Football players born in Kiel

Germany 55 players 1850–2007 50 men · 5 women

Kiel is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Andreas Köpke, but the city has produced 55 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Sidney Sam, Laura Freigang, Fin Bartels and Fabian Reese. Among them are 50 male and 5 female players. Documented players span more than 157 years, from 1850 to 2007.

Players from Kiel have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
