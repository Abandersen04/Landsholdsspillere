Burkina Faso 71 players 1966–2003 65 men · 6 women

This page lists all 71 football players born in Burkina Faso that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Issa Kaboré, Abdoul Tapsoba, Cyrille Bayala and Zakaria Sanogo. Among them are 65 male and 6 female players. Birth years range from 1966 to 2003.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
