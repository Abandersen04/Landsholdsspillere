Football players born in Amsterdam

Netherlands 841 players 1871–2009 809 men · 28 women

This page lists all 841 football players born in Amsterdam that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Johan Cruyff, Dennis Bergkamp, Louis van Gaal and Ruud Gullit. Among them are 809 male and 28 female players. Documented players span more than 138 years, from 1871 to 2009.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
