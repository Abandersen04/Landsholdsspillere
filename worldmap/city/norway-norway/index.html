Football players born in Norway

Norway 135 players 1901–2008 95 men · 40 women

Norway is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Frida Maanum, but the city has produced 135 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Bente Nordby, Elisabeth Terland, Amalie Eikeland and Julie Blakstad. Among them are 95 male and 40 female players. Documented players span more than 107 years, from 1901 to 2008.

Players from Norway have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
