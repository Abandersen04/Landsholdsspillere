Football players born in Eibar

Spain 44 players 1890–2002 39 men · 5 women

Eibar is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Mikel Oyarzabal, but the city has produced 44 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Markel Susaeta, José Muguerza, Ciriaco Errasti and Iñigo Ruiz de Galarreta. Among them are 39 male and 5 female players. Documented players span more than 112 years, from 1890 to 2002.
