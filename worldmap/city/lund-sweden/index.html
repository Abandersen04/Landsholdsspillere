Football players born in Lund

Sweden 26 players 1922–2006 24 men · 2 women

Lund is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Armin Gigović, but the city has produced 26 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Daniel Andersson, Andreas Jakobsson, Stellan Nilsson and Labinot Harbuzi. Among them are 24 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 84 years, from 1922 to 2006.
