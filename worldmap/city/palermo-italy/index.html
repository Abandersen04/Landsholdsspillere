Italy 165 players 1894–2004 162 men · 3 women

Palermo, Italy, is a significant source of football talent — 165 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Mario Balotelli, Salvatore Schillaci and Giuseppe Furino. Among them are 162 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 110 years, from 1894 to 2004.

The players listed here were identified through Wikidata's open knowledge base, where each person is classified as an association football player (Q937857) with Palermo registered as their birthplace. The data is community-maintained and updated continuously.
