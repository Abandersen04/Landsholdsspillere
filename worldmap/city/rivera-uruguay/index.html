Football players born in Rivera

Uruguay 47 players 1900–2005

Rivera is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Ronald Araújo, but the city has produced 47 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Mauricio Lemos, Pablo Bengoechea, Hugo de León and Rodrigo Mora. Documented players span more than 105 years, from 1900 to 2005.
