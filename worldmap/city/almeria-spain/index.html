Football players born in Almería

Spain 36 players 1899–2005

Almería is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Rubén Duarte Sánchez, but the city has produced 36 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Antonio Biosca, Francisco Javier Rodríguez Vílchez, José Ortiz Bernal and Daniel Romera Andújar. Documented players span more than 106 years, from 1899 to 2005.
