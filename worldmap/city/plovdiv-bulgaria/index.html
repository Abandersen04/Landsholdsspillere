Football players born in Plovdiv

Bulgaria 226 players 1895–2006 225 men · 1 women

Plovdiv is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Hristo Stoichkov, but the city has produced 226 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Hristo Bonev, Dinko Dermendzhiev, Ayan Sadakov and Todor Diev. Among them are 225 male and 1 female players. Documented players span more than 111 years, from 1895 to 2006.

Players from Plovdiv have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
