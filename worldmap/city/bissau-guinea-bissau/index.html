Football players born in Bissau

Guinea-Bissau 140 players 1944–2007

Bissau is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Ansu Fati, but the city has produced 140 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Éder, Danilo Pereira, Bruma and Esmaël Gonçalves. Birth years range from 1944 to 2007.
