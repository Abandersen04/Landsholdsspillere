Football players born in Ahvaz

Iran 71 players 1943–2006 69 men · 2 women

Ahvaz is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Pejman Montazeri, but the city has produced 71 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Ahmad Alenemeh, Naeim Saadavi, Mohammad Sadeghi and Reza Norouzi. Among them are 69 male and 2 female players. Birth years range from 1943 to 2006.

Players from Ahvaz have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
