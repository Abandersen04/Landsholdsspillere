Football players born in Gdańsk

Poland 98 players 1911–2007

Gdańsk is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Przemysław Frankowski, but the city has produced 98 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Andrzej Szarmach, Tomasz Wałdoch, Janusz Kupcewicz and Łukasz Szukała. Documented players span more than 96 years, from 1911 to 2007.
