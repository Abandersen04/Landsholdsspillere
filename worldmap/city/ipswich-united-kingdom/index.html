Football players born in Ipswich

United Kingdom 59 players 1882–2001

Ipswich is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Kieron Dyer, but the city has produced 59 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Richard Wright, Titus Bramble, Joe Morrell and Gary Bailey. Documented players span more than 119 years, from 1882 to 2001.

Players from Ipswich have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
