Kingdom of Prussia 81 players 1919–2003 77 men · 4 women

Szczecin, Kingdom of Prussia, is a notable football birthplace — 81 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Kamil Grosicki, Grzegorz Rasiak and Filip Starzyński. Among them are 77 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 84 years, from 1919 to 2003.
