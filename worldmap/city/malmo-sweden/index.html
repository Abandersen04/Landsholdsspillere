Football players born in Malmö

Sweden 144 players 1896–2006 138 men · 6 women

How many professional footballers have come from Malmö? According to Wikidata, at least 144 association football players were born here. That list includes Robin Olsen, Christian Wilhelmsson and Filip Helander. Among them are 138 male and 6 female players. Documented players span more than 110 years, from 1896 to 2006.

Players from Malmö have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
