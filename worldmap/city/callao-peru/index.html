Football players born in Callao

Peru 153 players 1850–2007 151 men · 2 women

Callao is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Claudio Pizarro, but the city has produced 153 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jesús Castillo, Yoshimar Yotún, Carlos Zambrano and Paolo Hurtado. Among them are 151 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 157 years, from 1850 to 2007.

Players from Callao have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
