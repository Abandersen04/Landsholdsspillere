This page lists all 132 football players born in Düsseldorf that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Klaus Allofs, Malick Thiaw, Rodolphe Seeldrayers and Amin Younes. Among them are 119 male and 13 female players. Documented players span more than 128 years, from 1876 to 2004.
