How many professional footballers have come from Baku? According to Wikidata, at least 283 association football players were born here. That list includes Yura Movsisyan, Anatoly Banishevsky and Rashad Sadygov. Among them are 277 male and 6 female players. Documented players span more than 102 years, from 1907 to 2009.

Players from Baku have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
