Football players born in Zagreb

Croatia 433 players 1888–2007 429 men · 4 women

This page lists all 433 football players born in Zagreb that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Marcelo Brozović, Andrej Kramarić, Niko Kranjčar and Joško Gvardiol. Among them are 429 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 119 years, from 1888 to 2007.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
