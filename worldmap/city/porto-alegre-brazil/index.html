Football players born in Porto Alegre

Brazil 289 players 1887–2008 281 men · 8 women

Porto Alegre is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Ronaldinho, but the city has produced 289 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Raphinha, Anderson, Roger Machado and Tinga. Among them are 281 male and 8 female players. Documented players span more than 121 years, from 1887 to 2008.

Players from Porto Alegre have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
