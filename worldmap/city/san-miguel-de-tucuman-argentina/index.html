Football players born in San Miguel de Tucumán

Argentina 125 players 1899–2004 124 men · 1 women

San Miguel de Tucumán is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Roberto Pereyra, but the city has produced 125 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Matías Kranevitter, Pablo Hernández, José Luis Palomino and Rafael Albrecht. Among them are 124 male and 1 female players. Documented players span more than 105 years, from 1899 to 2004.

Players from San Miguel de Tucumán have represented a wide range of national teams.
