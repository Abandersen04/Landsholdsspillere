Slovenia 157 players 1895–2004 148 men · 9 women

Ljubljana, Slovenia, is a significant source of football talent — 157 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Milivoje Novaković, Zlatan Ljubijankič and Branko Ilić. Among them are 148 male and 9 female players. Documented players span more than 109 years, from 1895 to 2004.

The players listed here were identified through Wikidata's open knowledge base, where each person is classified as an association football player (Q937857) with Ljubljana registered as their birthplace. The data is community-maintained and updated continuously.
