Coimbra is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Miguel Veloso, but the city has produced 51 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Francisco Conceição, Sérgio Conceição, Zé Castro and Carlos Jorge Neto Martins. Among them are 50 male and 1 female players. Documented players span more than 96 years, from 1906 to 2002.
