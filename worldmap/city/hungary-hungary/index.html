Hungary 70 players 1881–2004 63 men · 7 women

Hungary, Hungary, is a notable football birthplace — 70 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are István Tamássy, Gábor P. Szabó and János Bédl. Among them are 63 male and 7 female players. Documented players span more than 123 years, from 1881 to 2004.
