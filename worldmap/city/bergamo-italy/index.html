Bergamo, Italy, is a significant source of football talent — 116 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The players listed here were identified through Wikidata's open knowledge base, where each person is classified as an association football player (Q937857) with Bergamo registered as their birthplace. The data is community-maintained and updated continuously.
