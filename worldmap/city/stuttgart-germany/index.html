Germany 173 players 1879–2008 161 men · 12 women

How many professional footballers have come from Stuttgart? According to Wikidata, at least 173 association football players were born here. That list includes Jürgen Klopp, Sami Khedira and Timo Werner. Among them are 161 male and 12 female players. Documented players span more than 129 years, from 1879 to 2008.

Players from Stuttgart have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
