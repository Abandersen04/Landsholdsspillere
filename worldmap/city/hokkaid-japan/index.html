Football players born in Hokkaidō

Japan 102 players 1906–2009 95 men · 7 women

Hokkaidō is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Shigemitsu Sudo, but the city has produced 102 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Manami Nakano, Arisa Matsubara, Yusuke Minoguchi and Takuya Kawaguchi. Among them are 95 male and 7 female players. Documented players span more than 103 years, from 1906 to 2009.

Players from Hokkaidō have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
