Football players born in Pyongyang

North Korea 88 players 1912–2007 79 men · 9 women

Pyongyang, North Korea, is a notable football birthplace — 88 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Kim Sung-gan, Hong Yong-jo and Ri Myong-guk. Among them are 79 male and 9 female players. Documented players span more than 95 years, from 1912 to 2007.
