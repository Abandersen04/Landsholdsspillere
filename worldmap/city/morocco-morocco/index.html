Football players born in Morocco

Morocco 151 players 1918–2008 139 men · 7 women

This page lists all 151 football players born in Morocco that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Marwane Saâdane, Labid Khalifa, Abdallah Lamrani and Mohammed El Filali. Among them are 139 male and 7 female players. Documented players span more than 90 years, from 1918 to 2008.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
