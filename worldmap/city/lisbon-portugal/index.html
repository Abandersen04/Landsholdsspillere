Football players born in Lisbon

Portugal 491 players 1864–2009 477 men · 14 women

Lisbon, Portugal, is a significant source of football talent — 491 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Luís Figo, Ricardo Quaresma and Bernardo Silva. Among them are 477 male and 14 female players. Documented players span more than 145 years, from 1864 to 2009.

The players listed here were identified through Wikidata's open knowledge base, where each person is classified as an association football player (Q937857) with Lisbon registered as their birthplace. The data is community-maintained and updated continuously.
