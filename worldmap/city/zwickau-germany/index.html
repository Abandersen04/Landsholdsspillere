Germany 32 players 1920–2005 30 men · 2 women

Zwickau is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Christoph Daum, but the city has produced 32 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jürgen Croy, Gerhard Körner, Willy Tröger and Danny Röhl. Among them are 30 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 85 years, from 1920 to 2005.
