Football players born in Salamanca

Spain 51 players 1920–2003 49 men · 2 women

Salamanca is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Álvaro Arbeloa, but the city has produced 51 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Vicente del Bosque, Óscar González Marcos, Ibán Cuadrado and Julio Velázquez. Among them are 49 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 83 years, from 1920 to 2003.

Players from Salamanca have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
