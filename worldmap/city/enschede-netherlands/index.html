Football players born in Enschede

Netherlands 50 players 1906–2010 47 men · 3 women

Enschede is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Karim El Ahmadi, but the city has produced 50 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Remko Pasveer, Sander Westerveld, Tjaronn Chery and Jan Jeuring. Among them are 47 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 104 years, from 1906 to 2010.

Players from Enschede have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
