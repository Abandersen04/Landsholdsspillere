Football players born in Budapest

Hungary 1120 players 1873–2009 994 men · 126 women

Budapest, Hungary, is one of the world's most prolific football birthplaces — 1120 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Ferenc Puskás, Sándor Kocsis and Péter Gulácsi. Among them are 994 male and 126 female players. Documented players span more than 136 years, from 1873 to 2009.
