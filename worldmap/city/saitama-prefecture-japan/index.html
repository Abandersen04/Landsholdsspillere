Japan 308 players 1907–2006 287 men · 21 women

How many professional footballers have come from Saitama Prefecture? According to Wikidata, at least 308 association football players were born here. That list includes Ko Takamoro, Yoshio Kato and Koji Tanaka. Among them are 287 male and 21 female players. Documented players span more than 99 years, from 1907 to 2006.

Players from Saitama Prefecture have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
