Football players born in Villa María

Argentina 27 players 1902–2005

Villa María is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Mauro Rosales, but the city has produced 27 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Franco Jara, Germán Berterame, Enzo Barrenechea and Ángel Guillermo Hoyos. Documented players span more than 103 years, from 1902 to 2005.
