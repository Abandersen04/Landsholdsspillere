Football players born in Kanagawa Prefecture

Japan 397 players 1884–2012 370 men · 26 women

This page lists all 397 football players born in Kanagawa Prefecture that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Yūki Mutō, Masao Uchino, Yasuo Suzuki and Shuhei Terada. Among them are 370 male and 26 female players. Documented players span more than 128 years, from 1884 to 2012.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
