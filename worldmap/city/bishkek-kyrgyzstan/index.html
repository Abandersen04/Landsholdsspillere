Kyrgyzstan 79 players 1936–2004 75 men · 4 women

Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, is a notable football birthplace — 79 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Ilzat Akhmetov, Valery Kichin and Kairat Zhyrgalbek Uulu. Among them are 75 male and 4 female players. Birth years range from 1936 to 2004.
