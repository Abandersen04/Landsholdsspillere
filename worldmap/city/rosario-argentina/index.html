This page lists all 617 football players born in Rosario that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Lionel Messi, Ángel Di María, Mauro Icardi and Maxi Rodriguez. Among them are 609 male and 8 female players. Documented players span more than 207 years, from 1801 to 2008.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
