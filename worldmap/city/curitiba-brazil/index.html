Brazil 176 players 1898–2006 170 men · 6 women

This page lists all 176 football players born in Curitiba that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Thiago Neves, Adriano Correia Claro, Thiago Cionek and Alex de Souza. Among them are 170 male and 6 female players. Documented players span more than 108 years, from 1898 to 2006.
