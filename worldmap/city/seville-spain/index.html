Football players born in Seville

Spain 214 players 1887–2007 192 men · 22 women

Seville is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Carlos Marchena, but the city has produced 214 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Alberto Moreno, Antonio Puerta, Sergio Rico and Adrián. Among them are 192 male and 22 female players. Documented players span more than 120 years, from 1887 to 2007.
