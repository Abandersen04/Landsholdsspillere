Football players born in Shizuoka Prefecture

Japan 212 players 1894–2004 198 men · 14 women

This page lists all 212 football players born in Shizuoka Prefecture that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Tadao Horie, Naoto Otake, Yoshinori Ishigami and Masaru Uchiyama. Among them are 198 male and 14 female players. Documented players span more than 110 years, from 1894 to 2004.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
