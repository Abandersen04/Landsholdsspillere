Albania 63 players 1912–2002 60 men · 3 women

This page lists all 63 football players born in Durrës that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Orges Shehi, Panajot Pano, Edmond Kapllani and Renato Arapi. Among them are 60 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 90 years, from 1912 to 2002.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
