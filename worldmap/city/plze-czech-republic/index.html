Football players born in Plzeň

Czech Republic 86 players 1883–2000 81 men · 5 women

Plzeň is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Petr Čech, but the city has produced 86 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Vladimír Darida, David Limberský, Robin Hranáč and Lukáš Provod. Among them are 81 male and 5 female players. Documented players span more than 117 years, from 1883 to 2000.

Players from Plzeň have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
