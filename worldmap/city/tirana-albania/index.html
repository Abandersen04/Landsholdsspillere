Albania 284 players 1908–2003 282 men · 2 women

This page lists all 284 football players born in Tirana that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Ansi Agolli, Erjon Bogdani, Panagiotis Kone and Altin Lala. Among them are 282 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 95 years, from 1908 to 2003.
