Football players born in Como

Italy 72 players 1894–2008 66 men · 6 women

This page lists all 72 football players born in Como that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Gianluca Zambrotta, Gabriele Oriali, Patrick Cutrone and Gigi Meroni. Among them are 66 male and 6 female players. Documented players span more than 114 years, from 1894 to 2008.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
