Netherlands 27 players 1985–2006 25 men · 2 women

Almere is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Sergiño Dest, but the city has produced 27 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Hedwiges Maduro, Mike van der Hoorn, Desiree van Lunteren and Danny Holla. Among them are 25 male and 2 female players. Most players were born between 1985 and 2006.
