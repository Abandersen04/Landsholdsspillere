Netherlands 44 players 1885–2005 42 men · 2 women

Alkmaar is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Kenneth Taylor, but the city has produced 44 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Wesley Hoedt, Joshua John, Caius Welcker and Yusuf Barası. Among them are 42 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 120 years, from 1885 to 2005.
