Football players born in Poland

Poland 176 players 1912–2008 135 men · 41 women

How many professional footballers have come from Poland? According to Wikidata, at least 176 association football players were born here. That list includes Yisha'ayahu Schwager, Oskar Pietuszewski and Dominika Grabowska. Among them are 135 male and 41 female players. Documented players span more than 96 years, from 1912 to 2008.

Players from Poland have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
