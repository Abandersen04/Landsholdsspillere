Football players born in Giza

Egypt 29 players 1959–2000

Giza is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Ramadan Sobhi, but the city has produced 29 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Mostafa Mohamed, Mostafa Shobeir, Ismail Youssef and Abdel Halim Ali. Birth years range from 1959 to 2000.
