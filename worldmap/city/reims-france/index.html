France 43 players 1919–2005 36 men · 7 women

Reims, France, is a notable football birthplace — 43 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Robert Pires, Hugo Ekitike and Abdoulaye Diallo. Among them are 36 male and 7 female players. Documented players span more than 86 years, from 1919 to 2005.
