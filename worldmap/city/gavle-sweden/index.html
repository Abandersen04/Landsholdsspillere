Gävle, Sweden, is a notable football birthplace — 23 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Martin Olsson, Gunnar Jansson and Marcus Olsson. Among them are 22 male and 1 female players. Documented players span more than 105 years, from 1894 to 1999.
