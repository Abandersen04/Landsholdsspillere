Football players born in Iran

Iran 257 players 1921–2002 253 men · 4 women

This page lists all 257 football players born in Iran that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Alireza Ghashghaean, Ali Shojaei, Hamid Majd Teymouri and Sahameddin Mirfakhraei. Among them are 253 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 81 years, from 1921 to 2002.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
