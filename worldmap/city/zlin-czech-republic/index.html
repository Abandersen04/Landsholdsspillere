Czech Republic 39 players 1921–2007 37 men · 2 women

Zlín, Czech Republic, is a notable football birthplace — 39 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Ondřej Čelůstka, Svatopluk Pluskal and Petr Janečka. Among them are 37 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 86 years, from 1921 to 2007.
