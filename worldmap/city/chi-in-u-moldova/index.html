Moldova 199 players 1915–2006 192 men · 6 women

Chișinău, Moldova, is a significant source of football talent — 199 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Alexandru Epureanu, Alexandru Gațcan and Artur Ioniță. Among them are 192 male and 6 female players. Documented players span more than 91 years, from 1915 to 2006.
