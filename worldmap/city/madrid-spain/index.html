Football players born in Madrid

Spain 803 players 1883–2018 736 men · 67 women

Madrid, Spain, is one of the world's most prolific football birthplaces — 803 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Raúl, David de Gea and Pepe Reina. Among them are 736 male and 67 female players. Documented players span more than 135 years, from 1883 to 2018.
