Ukraine 182 players 1891–2007 180 men · 1 women

This page lists all 182 football players born in Kharkiv that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Volodymyr Bezsonov, Maksym Kalynychenko, Oleksandr Horyainov and Valeriy Bondar. Among them are 180 male and 1 female players. Documented players span more than 116 years, from 1891 to 2007.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
