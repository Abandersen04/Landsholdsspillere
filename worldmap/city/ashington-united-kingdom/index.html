Football players born in Ashington

United Kingdom 57 players 1887–2000

Ashington is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Bobby Charlton, but the city has produced 57 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jack Charlton, Jackie Milburn, Martin Taylor and Jimmy Adamson. Documented players span more than 113 years, from 1887 to 2000.

Players from Ashington have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
