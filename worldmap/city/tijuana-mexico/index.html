Football players born in Tijuana

Mexico 36 players 1950–2007 30 men · 6 women

Tijuana is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Héctor Herrera, but the city has produced 36 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jorge Torres de Marfil, Fernando Arce, Víctor Guzmán and Moisés Velasco. Among them are 30 male and 6 female players. Birth years range from 1950 to 2007.
