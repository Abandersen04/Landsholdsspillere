Pittsburgh, United States, is a notable football birthplace — 28 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Marvell Wynne, Meghan Klingenberg and Mike Bookie. Among them are 25 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 112 years, from 1892 to 2004.
