New Zealand 23 players 1856–2003 15 men · 8 women

Christchurch is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Ryan Nelsen, but the city has produced 23 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Aaron Clapham, Finn Surman, Jeremy Brockie and Bill Tuiloma. Among them are 15 male and 8 female players. Documented players span more than 147 years, from 1856 to 2003.
