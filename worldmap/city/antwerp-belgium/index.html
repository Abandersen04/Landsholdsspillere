How many professional footballers have come from Antwerp? According to Wikidata, at least 129 association football players were born here. Players from Antwerp have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
