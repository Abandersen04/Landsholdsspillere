Football players born in Brasília

Brazil 108 players 1964–2004 100 men · 8 women

Brasília is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Lúcio, but the city has produced 108 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Washington Stecanela Cerqueira, Márcio Amoroso, Carlos Alberto Dias and Leandro Moura. Among them are 100 male and 8 female players. Birth years range from 1964 to 2004.
