Football players born in Helsingborg

Sweden 62 players 1890–2002 60 men · 2 women

This page lists all 62 football players born in Helsingborg that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Henrik Larsson, Andreas Granqvist, Roland Nilsson and Alexander Kačaniklić. Among them are 60 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 112 years, from 1890 to 2002.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
