Football players born in Dubrovnik

Croatia 37 players 1901–2007

Dubrovnik is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Emir Spahić, but the city has produced 37 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Alen Halilović, Srđan Lakić, Božo Broketa and Andro Bušlje. Documented players span more than 106 years, from 1901 to 2007.
