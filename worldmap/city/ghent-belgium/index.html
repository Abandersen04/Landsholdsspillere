Belgium 75 players 1854–2008 73 men · 2 women

Ghent, Belgium, is a notable football birthplace — 75 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Vadis Odjidja-Ofoe, Olivier Deschacht and Benito Raman. Among them are 73 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 154 years, from 1854 to 2008.
