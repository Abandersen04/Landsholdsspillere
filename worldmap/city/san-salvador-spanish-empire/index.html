San Salvador, Spanish Empire, is a significant source of football talent — 115 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Jaime Rodríguez Jiménez, Mágico González and José María Rivas. Among them are 113 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 98 years, from 1901 to 1999.
