Brazil 217 players 1900–2005 212 men · 4 women

This page lists all 217 football players born in Belo Horizonte that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Toninho Cerezo, Tostão, Fred and Juan Jesus. Among them are 212 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 105 years, from 1900 to 2005.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
