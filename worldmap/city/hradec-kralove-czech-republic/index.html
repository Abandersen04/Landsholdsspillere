Football players born in Hradec Králové

Czech Republic 49 players 1895–2004 45 men · 4 women

Hradec Králové is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Tomáš Koubek, but the city has produced 49 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Václav Němeček, Jaroslav Zelený, Martin Frýdek and Karel Piták. Among them are 45 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 109 years, from 1895 to 2004.
