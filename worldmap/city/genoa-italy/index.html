Italy 288 players 1871–2006 278 men · 10 women

How many professional footballers have come from Genoa? According to Wikidata, at least 288 association football players were born here. That list includes Federico Chiesa, Enrico Chiesa and Gian Piero Ventura. Among them are 278 male and 10 female players. Documented players span more than 135 years, from 1871 to 2006.

Players from Genoa have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
