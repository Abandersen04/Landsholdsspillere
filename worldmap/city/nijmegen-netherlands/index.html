Football players born in Nijmegen

Netherlands 107 players 1873–2007 101 men · 5 women

Nijmegen is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Jasper Cillessen, but the city has produced 107 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Eloy Room, Piet Velthuizen, Erik van Rossum and Dennis Gentenaar. Among them are 101 male and 5 female players. Documented players span more than 134 years, from 1873 to 2007.
