People's Republic of China 62 players 1954–2003 35 men · 27 women

This page lists all 62 football players born in People's Republic of China that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Fu Ming, Zhao Lina, Zhang Yan and Pan Lina. The dataset includes a notably high share of female players: 27 women and 35 men. Birth years range from 1954 to 2003.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
