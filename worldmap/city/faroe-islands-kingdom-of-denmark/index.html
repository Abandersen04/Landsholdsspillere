Football in Faroe Islands has deep roots — the earliest documented player in this dataset was born in 1960. In total, 56 players from Faroe Islands, Kingdom of Denmark, appear on Wikidata. The players listed here were identified through Wikidata's open knowledge base, where each person is classified as an association football player (Q937857) with Faroe Islands registered as their birthplace. The data is community-maintained and updated continuously.
