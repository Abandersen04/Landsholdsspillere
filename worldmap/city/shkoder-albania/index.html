This page lists all 128 football players born in Shkodër that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Rudi Vata, Elseid Hisaj, Bekim Balaj and Hamdi Salihi. Among them are 121 male and 6 female players. Documented players span more than 96 years, from 1908 to 2004.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
