Football players born in Bunkyō-ku

Japan 6 players 1909–1996

Bunkyō-ku is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Koichi Oita, but the city has produced 6 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Teiichi Matsumaru, Hiroto Muraoka, Yukio Tsuchiya and Shunsuke Motegi. Documented players span more than 87 years, from 1909 to 1996.
