This page lists all 102 football players born in Lanús that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Dani Osvaldo, Alejandro Domínguez, Hugo Maradona and Omar Larrosa. Among them are 98 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 106 years, from 1902 to 2008.
