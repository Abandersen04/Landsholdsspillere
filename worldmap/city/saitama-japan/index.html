Football players born in Saitama

Japan 104 players 1931–2005 100 men · 4 women

Saitama is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Eiji Kawashima, but the city has produced 104 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Yasuharu Sorimachi, Takashi Takabayashi, Shinji Tanaka and Yoshikazu Nagai. Among them are 100 male and 4 female players. Birth years range from 1931 to 2005.
