Football players born in Kragujevac

Serbia 103 players 1902–2006 100 men · 3 women

Kragujevac is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Filip Kostić, but the city has produced 103 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Luka Milivojević, Danko Lazović, Predrag Đorđević and Nenad Tomović. Among them are 100 male and 3 female players. Documented players span more than 104 years, from 1902 to 2006.
