Football players born in Argentina

Argentina 546 players 1801–2004 530 men · 16 women

How many professional footballers have come from Argentina? According to Wikidata, at least 546 association football players were born here. That list includes Marcelo Trivisonno, Sergio Escudero and Rodrigo Riep. Among them are 530 male and 16 female players. Documented players span more than 203 years, from 1801 to 2004.

Players from Argentina have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
