Football players born in Derby

United Kingdom 162 players 1860–2007 160 men · 2 women

Derby is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Jamaal Lascelles, but the city has produced 162 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jordon Mutch, Mark Hateley, Lee Camp and Stephy Mavididi. Among them are 160 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 147 years, from 1860 to 2007.

Players from Derby have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
