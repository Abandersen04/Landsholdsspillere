Football players born in Kampen

Netherlands 12 players 1880–1987

Kampen is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Jaap Stam, but the city has produced 12 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Henrico Drost, Oğuzhan Türk, Jeroen Drost and Henry van der Vegt. Documented players span more than 107 years, from 1880 to 1987.
