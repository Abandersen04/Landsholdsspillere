Ukraine 237 players 1878–2005 236 men · 1 women

This page lists all 237 football players born in Lviv that are documented on Wikidata. The best-known names from the city include Roman Yaremchuk, Oleh Luzhnyi, Volodymyr Yezerskyi and Kazimierz Górski. Among them are 236 male and 1 female players. Documented players span more than 127 years, from 1878 to 2005.

Click any player's name to read their Wikipedia article. The notability score (the blue badge) reflects global Wikipedia coverage: a score of 100 corresponds to Lionel Messi's 223 language editions, while most players score between 15 and 50.
