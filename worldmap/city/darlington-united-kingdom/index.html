Football players born in Darlington

United Kingdom 89 players 1858–2001

Darlington is best known in football circles as the birthplace of James Morrison, but the city has produced 89 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Bailey Peacock-Farrell, Giuseppe Wilson, Ronald Brebner and Harry Catterick. Documented players span more than 143 years, from 1858 to 2001.
