Poland 39 players 1915–2006 38 men · 1 women

Nowy Sącz is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Piotr Świerczewski, but the city has produced 39 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Jakub Słowik, Aleksander Kłak, Dawid Janczyk and Janusz Kowalik. Among them are 38 male and 1 female players. Documented players span more than 91 years, from 1915 to 2006.
