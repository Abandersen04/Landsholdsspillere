Football players born in Niigata Prefecture

Japan 36 players 1960–2006 31 men · 5 women

Niigata Prefecture is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Kenji Komata, but the city has produced 36 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Tomoaki Seino, Kento Tsurumaki, Goson Sakai and Takumi Hasegawa. Among them are 31 male and 5 female players. Birth years range from 1960 to 2006.
