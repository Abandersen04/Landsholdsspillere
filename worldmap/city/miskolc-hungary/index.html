Football players born in Miskolc

Hungary 86 players 1901–2009 79 men · 7 women

Miskolc, Hungary, is a notable football birthplace — 86 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Szabolcs Huszti, Mihály Korhut and Bálint Vécsei. Among them are 79 male and 7 female players. Documented players span more than 108 years, from 1901 to 2009.
