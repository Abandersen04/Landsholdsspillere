Sremska Mitrovica, Serbia, is a notable football birthplace — 28 association football players on Wikidata list the city as their place of birth. The most notable are Branislav Ivanović, Marko Petković and Goran Stevanović. Documented players span more than 94 years, from 1907 to 2001.
