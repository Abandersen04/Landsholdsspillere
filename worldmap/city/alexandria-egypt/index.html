Football players born in Alexandria

Egypt 35 players 1890–1999

Alexandria is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Amr Warda, but the city has produced 35 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Gedo, Ad-Diba, Ibrahim Said and Shady Mohamed. Documented players span more than 109 years, from 1890 to 1999.
