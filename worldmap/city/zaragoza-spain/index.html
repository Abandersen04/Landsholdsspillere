Football players born in Zaragoza

Spain 152 players 1894–2009 139 men · 11 women

Zaragoza is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Jesús Vallejo, but the city has produced 152 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Salma Paralluelo, Mapi León, Ignacio Camacho and Víctor Muñoz. Among them are 139 male and 11 female players. Documented players span more than 115 years, from 1894 to 2009.
