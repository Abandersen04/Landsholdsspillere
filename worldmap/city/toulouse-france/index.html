Football players born in Toulouse

France 119 players 1897–2008 107 men · 12 women

Toulouse is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Blaise Matuidi, but the city has produced 119 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Philippe Mexès, Gaël Clichy, Liassine Cadamuro and Daniel Bravo. Among them are 107 male and 12 female players. Documented players span more than 111 years, from 1897 to 2008.
