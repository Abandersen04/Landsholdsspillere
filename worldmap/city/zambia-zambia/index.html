Football players born in Zambia

Zambia 139 players 1946–2006 113 men · 26 women

Zambia is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Patson Daka, but the city has produced 139 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Fashion Sakala, Kabange Mupopo, Joshua Titima and Danny Munyau. Among them are 113 male and 26 female players. Birth years range from 1946 to 2006.

Players from Zambia have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
