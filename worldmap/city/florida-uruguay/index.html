Football players born in Florida

Uruguay 37 players 1914–2003

Florida is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Jonathan Rodríguez, but the city has produced 37 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Sebastián Viera, Atilio Ancheta, Brian Ocampo and Martín Ligüera. Documented players span more than 89 years, from 1914 to 2003.
