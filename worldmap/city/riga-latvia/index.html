How many professional footballers have come from Riga? According to Wikidata, at least 281 association football players were born here. That list includes Vitālijs Astafjevs, Andrejs Rubins and Kaspars Gorkšs. Among them are 279 male and 2 female players. Documented players span more than 113 years, from 1895 to 2008.

Players from Riga have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
