Football players born in Aichi Prefecture

Japan 137 players 1914–2006 127 men · 10 women

How many professional footballers have come from Aichi Prefecture? According to Wikidata, at least 137 association football players were born here. That list includes Taizo Kawamoto, Mitsunori Yoshida and Shinichiro Tani. Among them are 127 male and 10 female players. Documented players span more than 92 years, from 1914 to 2006.

Players from Aichi Prefecture have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
