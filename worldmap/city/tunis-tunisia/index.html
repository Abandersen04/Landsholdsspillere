Football players born in Tunis

Tunisia 200 players 1898–2005 196 men · 4 women

Tunis is best known in football circles as the birthplace of Ziad Tlemçani, but the city has produced 200 documented association football players in total. Other notable players include Yassine Meriah, Aymen Mathlouthi, Fakhreddine Ben Youssef and Oussama Haddadi. Among them are 196 male and 4 female players. Documented players span more than 107 years, from 1898 to 2005.

Players from Tunis have represented a wide range of national teams. The notability score shown next to each player is based on the number of Wikipedia language editions that have an article about them — a global proxy for how well-known the player is across different football cultures.
